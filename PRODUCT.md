# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Boards and leadership teams at tech-driven businesses across Africa. They arrive via referral or vendor research — which means they already have a reason to believe Movara might be credible. They are evaluating: is this the right partner for the kind of work we actually need done?

They are not browsing. They are deciding. The context is typically a laptop, during working hours, in a professional setting. The mood is measured: open but skeptical, pressed for time, allergic to pitch-speak.

The job to be done: confirm that Movara understands their specific problem (navigating disruption, stress-testing a strategy, executing with discipline) and has done it before for someone like them.

## Product Purpose

Movara is a strategy consulting firm helping tech-driven businesses across Africa prepare for disruption and execute with discipline. The site exists to convert a credible first impression into a consultation booking.

Success looks like a visitor who arrived uncertain leaving with the feeling: "This is the partner I've been looking for" — and acting on it.

## Brand Personality

**Bold, Practical, African.**

- **Bold**: takes a position, doesn't hedge. The copy speaks like someone who has been in the room when things went wrong and knows what that feels like.
- **Practical**: no abstraction for its own sake. Everything points toward real decisions, real timelines, real accountability.
- **African**: confident about where it operates and who it serves, without using African identity as decoration. Not pan-African color palettes, not Adinkra patterns as wallpaper. The African-ness is in the posture, the context, the specific problems named.

Voice tone: warm-editorial. A well-produced strategy report that a person actually wrote. Not a pitch deck. Not a brochure.

## Anti-references

Avoid all three categories explicitly and equally:

- **McKinsey-clone**: navy backgrounds, gold rule lines, corporate stock photos of boardrooms, anonymous suit-wearing silhouettes. The aesthetic of authority borrowed from old-world institutions.
- **SaaS startup landing page**: hero metrics, glowing gradients, "10x your growth" headlines, identical icon-grid feature sections, glassmorphism cards. The aesthetic of growth-at-all-costs.
- **Generic local African agency site**: bright primary colors (red, green, yellow at full saturation), busy decorative patterns, clip-art-style icons, testimonial carousels with thumbnail headshots, confetti-style section dividers.

The shared failure across all three: the design does the talking instead of the work. Movara's design should amplify the work, never substitute for it.

## Design Principles

1. **Earn trust before asking for a meeting.** The site's primary job is to make visitors feel understood before they are asked to act. Every section should reflect the visitor's problem back to them before presenting the solution. The CTA earns its right to appear.

2. **The work speaks; the site supports.** Credibility comes from outcomes (reducing non-performing loans from 70% to under 10%, stress-testing plans before the market does). Design frames the work. It never performs in place of it.

3. **African without quotation marks.** The identity is African by nature, not by decoration. Confidence about where Movara operates lives in the copy, the problems named, the geography acknowledged — not in visual shorthand.

4. **Practical over theatrical.** Workshops, wargaming, execution plans: this is hands-on consulting. The site should feel like you could roll up your sleeves here. Theatrical motion and decorative complexity undermine that.

5. **One ask per section.** A single primary CTA visible at any given scroll depth. The consultation booking should feel like an invitation, not a funnel. Scarcity of ask signals confidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Not a current priority for formal compliance. Flag WCAG 2.1 AA violations as they arise (color contrast, keyboard navigation, touch targets) and resolve them opportunistically. Touch-target minimums (44–48px) are already implemented in the design system and should be maintained.
